# Player frames

Neutral still frames the mock Plex server turns into the playable demo video
(`mock_plex.py`, `ensure_media`). The App Store screenshots never show real
scenes from the listed films and shows; the player plays these instead.
All are NASA imagery in the public domain (US federal government work, see the
[NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/));
no insignia or people appear and no endorsement is implied. Cropped to 16:9.
